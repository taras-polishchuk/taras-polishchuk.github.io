How I work

Contracts first. Guards around every model call. Nothing ships unobserved.

Schema at every boundary

Every LLM output is validated (Zod, JSON schema) before it touches a database, a CRM or a customer. Agents get explicit contracts with handoff rules.

Human approval where money or reputation is at stake

Outbound messages, payments and deletions wait for a tap in Telegram or the dashboard. Everything else runs on its own.

Defense in depth for prompts

Pre-LLM filters, post-LLM guards, allowlisted links and amounts, regression suites against documented threat vectors.

Self-hosted when it matters

Docker, Caddy, Cloudflare Tunnel and Access, Coolify, Proxmox, Infisical. Your data and your models stay where you decide.

Small production pilot, then scale

The first deliverable goes live within weeks. What proves itself gets expanded; what does not gets cut.

AI-assisted, human-reviewed

I use Claude Code, Codex and Copilot daily with manual review of every change. Tests and CI gates are part of the deliverable.
